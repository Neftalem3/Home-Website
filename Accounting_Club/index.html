UST Accounting Club

Empowering future accountants at the University of St. Thomas

Interested in Accounting? Whether you're a freshman just starting to explore the field or a senior preparing to launch your career, the St. Thomas Accounting Club is here to support you every step of the way.

From explore events and info sessions designed for underclassmen to networking opportunities, Big 4 panels, and recruiting prep for juniors and seniors, our events are tailored to help you build confidence, connections, and clarity in your future.

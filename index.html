Who we are

Two builders, one mission.

We're two college students who love building things on the web. We noticed that a lot of non-profits and student clubs either have no website at all, or one that hasn't been touched since 2011.

We started BuildForGood to fix that. We use modern tools to build fast, clean websites that help organizations actually get found, get donations, and get members.

We're not an agency. We're two people who care about this stuff — which means you get real attention, fast communication, and a site you'll actually be proud of.
